# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers and power users on macOS who already use Homebrew and look for useful native CLI tools and desktop apps. They value speed, terminal-first workflows, and apps that respect the platform. They skim, scan, and copy commands — they do not read marketing prose.

## Product Purpose

This is a personal Homebrew tap that distributes six small macOS tools (2 formulae, 4 casks). The site is the landing surface — its job is to let a visitor understand what is in the tap, copy the exact install command, and move on. The page must feel native to the Homebrew ecosystem: terminal, plain text, dependable.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, terminal-native, dependable. Three words: **calm · mechanical · honest**.

## Anti-references

- SaaS landing pages with hero metrics, gradient text, glass cards, and floating badges.
- Editorial-magazine aesthetics (Klim-style display serifs, rule-separated columns, italic drop caps) — overused in 2026 AI output and wrong for a developer-facing tap.
- Marketing copy that oversells six small CLI utilities as if they were a platform.
- Decorative motion, hero animations, page-load reveals that gate content visibility.

## Design Principles

1. Practice what the tap preaches — terminal-first, monospace-led, no decorative chrome.
2. Information density over whitespace theater. Six packages deserve six scannable rows, not a grid of identical cards.
3. Copy a command in one click; do not make visitors hunt for the install string.
4. Every visual choice must earn its place; restraint is voice, not absence of design.
5. Accessible by default — keyboard nav, focus rings, reduced-motion respect.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Reduced-motion respected. Focus rings visible. Copy buttons keyboard-operable. Color contrast verified for body text and command text on dark surface.
